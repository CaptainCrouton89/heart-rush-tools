## Action Surge

_Major ability. Martial talent._

You push your body and mind beyond normal limits, moving with impossible speed and precision. When you use this ability during the action phase, immediately gain an additional action that must be used this round. This action follows all normal rules and restrictions, and you may use other major abilities with this extra action.

**Destiny Level:**
When you use Action Surge, you also immediately gain 3 rush points. You can spend these rush points only on minor abilities, opportunity attacks, or sidesteps.
